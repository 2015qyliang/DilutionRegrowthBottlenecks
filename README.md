# DilutionRegrowthBottlenecks

## Stochastic founder-entry signatures and deterministic-enriched endpoint regimes shape microbial community reassembly after dilution bottlenecks

Authors: Qi-Yun Liang, Wen-Xing Yu, Ke-Lei Zhu, Guan-Jun Chen, Da-Shuai Mu*, Zong-Jun Du* 

The related scripts, raw data, and intermediate data are available. 

**Qi-Yun Liang** generated all experimental data during their **PhD candidacy (2019–2023)** at the Marine College, Shandong University at Weihai, China. These data correspond to Chapters 2 and 3 of the PhD thesis.

The citation of the PhD thesis is as follows: **梁其云.(2023).The Contributions of Marinilabiliales in Microbial Community Succession during Sediment Enrichment Cultures Process(博士学位论文,山东大学).博士https://doi.org/10.27272/d.cnki.gshdu.2023.007434.**
